Recall now records a dedicated video player's window without its image while the player is playing, even when it plays in a window rather than fullscreen. The record keeps its app, window title and time, and windowed playback is marked `video_playback` (fullscreen playback is still marked `fullscreen_video`). While a player is playing in one window, its other windows are recorded without images too. The players are Infuse, IINA, VLC, QuickTime Player, Apple TV, mpv, Plex, Plex HTPC, Movist and Movist Pro. Paused players, browsers and other apps are captured as before.
