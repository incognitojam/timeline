Remote Access can make Timeline's MCP endpoint reachable from the internet for 5 minutes, 1 hour or 1 day, with **Make Public on the Internet** in the menu bar app; other public requests are refused. While it is public, the menu says so and shows when it ends; **Stop Public Access** ends it early. It also ends when you turn Remote Access off, when you quit Timeline, when the Mac restarts, and at launch or wake once the time has passed.
