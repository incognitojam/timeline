Discord messages built with Components V2 now appear in conversations as cards with their text, thumbnails, galleries, files and separators, instead of as empty messages. Buttons and menus are shown but can't be clicked, and images whose Discord links have expired show a placeholder. Newly synced messages are searchable by their card text. Conversations, exports and assistant tools show card text on existing messages too, and existing messages become searchable after their stored text is re-rendered.
