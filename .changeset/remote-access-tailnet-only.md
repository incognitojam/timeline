Remote Access now serves Timeline to your tailnet only, with Tailscale Serve, and never to the public internet. After updating, a node that was set up with Funnel switches to tailnet only on its own, without logging in again. Hosted clients such as Claude.ai custom connectors can no longer reach the service. The menu shows "Remote Access: tailnet only" and offers **Copy Tailnet-Only URL**.
