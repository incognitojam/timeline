Choosing a contact on the People page shows their name and avatar straight away, with placeholders where their details and recent events will go, instead of keeping the previous contact on screen until the new one loads. The avatar and name glide from the list into the contact header in browsers that support view transitions (unless reduced motion is on), the tab title names the contact, hovering a contact starts loading them, and returning to the page scrolls the list to the selected contact. A contact whose photo loads now shows it in the header even when the previous contact's photo failed to load.
