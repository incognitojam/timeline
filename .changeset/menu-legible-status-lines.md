The menu bar app's status lines, such as "Remote Access: public until 18:15" and "Last capture 3 sec ago", are drawn at full contrast instead of greyed out, and clicking one does something useful: a Remote Access line or the address copies the URL, a recall line opens the recall source page, the version copies the version, and service, Remote Access and update problems open the logs folder. Action items show their icons on macOS 27.
