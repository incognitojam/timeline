Agents can now ask about a contact you have hidden. Hiding someone still keeps them out of what an agent is shown without asking, such as catch-ups, searches and the contact list, which now says how many hidden contacts it left out. But an agent that names them, by name, id or identifier, or opens one of their messages by id, gets their profile, messages and conversation like anyone else's, and `get_contact_info` says they are hidden.
