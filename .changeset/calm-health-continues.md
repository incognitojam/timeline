Keep steps, activity, active zone minutes, and weight updating from Google Health after the Fitbit Web API shuts down. On dates Google Health covers, the health dashboard and day panel use its values; earlier Fitbit records are kept. Google Health step counts combine the watch with phone step counting, so they can be higher than Fitbit's on days the watch was not worn all day. VO2 max is not available from Google Health.
