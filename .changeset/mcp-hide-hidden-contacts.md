Contacts marked hidden are now left out of Timeline's MCP tools, as they are on the web app's timeline pages. `list_contacts` no longer lists them, and `get_contact_info`, `query_events` and `save_memory_note` answer a hidden contact's name, id or identifier as they do an unknown one. Keyword search, date browsing, `get_context`, `export_conversation`, `semantic_search` (with any conversation window that holds, forwards or mentions one of their messages) and conversation grouping leave out the events filed under a hidden contact: a DM with them disappears, and a group chat keeps everyone else's messages, with no quotes or forwards of their messages; where a visible message mentions them, or they voted in a poll, they appear as "someone". `get_updates` now also leaves out forwards of their messages and calls them "someone" where a visible message mentions them. `get_schema` with a source shows its sample's value types instead of its values while any contact is hidden, since a reply's raw data can embed the message it answers. Memory notes linked to a hidden contact are left out of `list_memory`, `get_memory_note` and the connect-time memory index, and `delete_memory_note` treats them as missing. `run_sql` still reads every row, and `search_mcp_tool_calls` replays earlier results.
